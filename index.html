Things to try in the chat

  • Give short or vague answers (“ok”, “dunno”) — it re-asks once with options, then moves on.
  • Ask “how much?” or “are you a bot?” halfway — it answers honestly and comes back to the question.
  • Push back (“why should I trust you”) twice — it offers a person instead of pushing.
  • Say “can I talk to a real person” — it hands over with a summary, so you don't have to repeat yourself.
  • Volunteer details early (“12 pax, budget 20k, need it ASAP”) — it won't ask for them again.
